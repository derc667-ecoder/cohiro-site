Privacy policy

This covers the CoHiro app. The niugio.com website policy is separate and covers only that website.

Last updated: 31 July 2026.

The short version

CoHiro stores your email address, an optional display name, and whatever you and the people you live with put into your shared list and inventory. It is kept in Germany. There is no advertising, no tracking, no profiling, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

Three things are worth knowing before you read further, because they surprise people: notifications quote what you typed, your display name defaults to part of your email address, and deleting your account does not delete what you added to a shared household. Each is explained below.

2. What we store, and why

Your account

Your email address, because it is how you sign in and how we send you a code if you forget your password. Your password is never stored: only a cryptographic hash of it, which cannot be turned back into your password.

A display name, which is optional. If you do not choose one, we use the part of your email address before the @ sign. So if you sign up as firstname.lastname@example.com and skip the name field, the people in your household will see firstname.lastname. You can change it at any time in the app under Account.

What you and your household put in

The things the app is for: item names, shopping-list lines, notes you attach to them, the names of your shelves and of your household, whether something is in stock or running low, and a history of who changed what. All of it is free text, so it contains whatever you type.

This is a shared app, and that is the point. Everything you add to a household is visible to every other member of it, including your display name against the things you added. Do not put anything in a note that you would not want the people you live with to read.

Your devices

If you allow notifications, we store a push token for that device so we can reach it, along with which platform it is. We also store your time zone, so that "the 6pm shop" means 6pm where you are, and your quiet hours if you set them.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos, or your advertising identifier. The camera is used only to read a QR code when joining a household; no image is stored or transmitted.

3. Notifications quote what you typed

When someone starts a shop or calls one off, the people in the household get a notification. The household name is the title and any note the person wrote is quoted in the body.

To deliver it, that text passes through Expo's push service and then through Apple's or Google's notification service, and it can appear on a locked screen. This is how push notifications work on every app, but it means the text you type into a note is not confined to Germany and is not confined to the app. Keep that in mind when writing one.

4. Crash reports

When the app fails, it sends a crash report to Sentry so the fault can be fixed. The report contains the error, where in the code it happened, and technical details about the device such as its model and operating system version.

It does not contain your email address, your name, or anything you typed into the app. The name of your device is removed before the report is sent, because people often name their phone after themselves.

Crash reporting cannot currently be turned off. It is the only way we learn that something is broken, and it carries nothing that identifies you.

We do not use any analytics or tracking product. There is no advertising in CoHiro and no profiling of any kind.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app under Account. There is no need to ask us. Full instructions are on the account deletion page.

What is deleted: your email address, your display name, your settings, and the record of your devices.

What is not deleted: if you shared a household with other people, the things you added to it stay, because they are still using them. What changes is that those entries stop carrying your name. If you were the only person in a household, it is deleted with your account.

7. How long we keep things

Your account and its contents are kept until you delete them. There is no automatic expiry, and we do not delete inactive accounts. The household activity history is kept for two years and then removed automatically.

8. Children

CoHiro is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how it is used, or to object to its use. Most of that you can do yourself in the app; for anything else, write to support@cohiro.app.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. For us that is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will say so in the app rather than quietly updating this page. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
