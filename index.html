CoHiro. Shared grocery list.

What it does

One list, every phone

Add something and it is on everyone's phone straight away. When someone is at the shops, the rest of you watch it happen, so nobody buys the milk twice and nobody has to send a photo of a scrap of paper.

An inventory that speaks up

Keep a simple picture of what you have: in stock, running low, or out. Anything that runs low or out puts itself on the shopping list, without anyone remembering to do it. That is the part that makes the list trustworthy.

Shopping, coordinated

Say you are heading to the shops and everyone gets a heads-up in time to add what they need. Or ask whether anyone can go, and let someone volunteer. When the shopping is put away, the inventory updates itself.

Built for a household, not a person

Invite people into your household, wherever they live, and every member has the same rights: anyone can add, anyone can shop, anyone can invite. You can belong to more than one household, which is what a flatshare and a family actually look like.

Where your data lives

In Frankfurt, Germany, with a European provider. CoHiro has no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party requests on this website at all. You can delete your account yourself, from inside the app, and it really is deleted. The privacy policy says exactly what is stored and why, in plain language.
